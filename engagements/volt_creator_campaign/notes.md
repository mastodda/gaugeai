# VOLT Creator Campaign — Engagement Notes

## Stakeholder
Insights & Analytics Manager at HardScope (creator economy). Not a CPG buyer. The reframe: SSR as **pre-launch creative-angle screening** — the one decision his world makes but can't test cheaply.

## Context
Built for a discovery call. Use as illustrative material only if he asks to see it; otherwise lead with discovery questions.

## Why an energy drink
Heavy creator-marketing category, Gen-Z/young-millennial audience, clear angle differentiation. Same product across 4 concepts → clean monadic test.

## Decisions made
- **Panel size 80** — enough for stable rankings without burning credits on a demo.
- **Tier 2 personas** — qualitative "why" is the headline; richer personas produce more usable quotes.
- **Default reference sets + purchase_intent type** — stays on the validated path. Surface wording adjusted to "Imagine you saw this campaign from a creator you follow…" but underlying scoring is the validated PI gradient.
- **Skewed young (18–34), modest income** — target consumer for creator-led campaigns, not US-general.
- **seed 42** — reproducible.

## Caveats to flag in the deliverable
- Present **rankings**, not absolute PI scores. Known +1.0 positivity bias on PI.
- Do NOT cite the 90% correlation figure — that's Maier's, not our data.
- Synthetic/illustrative — not a real client study. Label clearly.
- If 3+ angles tie in the dry-run, the demo is weak — flag and rethink the angles.
- If persona generator rejects `nonbinary` (not in validated gender set), drop and renormalize woman/man. Do not patch the generator.

## Demo-day framing
- Open the explorer on **Tab 2 (Insights)** and **Tab 5 (Responses)** — qualitative "why angle X lands, why Y flops" is what reads as *insight*. PI math is secondary.
- Framing line: "Ranking-based directional read, ~a day, synthetic panel — not a replacement for a YouGov study, a cheap filter before one."
