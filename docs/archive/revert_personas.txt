REVERTING TO BASIC (TIER 1) PERSONAS
=====================================

Context:
Tier 2 rich personas produce lower absolute mean PI scores (~0.5 drop) compared
to the paper-validated Tier 1 template prompts. Concept ranking is preserved.
The drop likely reflects more realistic, grounded responses rather than scoring
degradation — but Tier 1 is the only method validated against human baselines
in the Maier et al. paper.

How to revert:
Remove (or rename) lifestyle_attributes.json from your config directory.
That's it. The pipeline auto-detects this file. Without it, persona generation
falls back to Tier 1 template-based prompts with no extra LLM calls.

How to re-enable:
Put lifestyle_attributes.json back in the config directory next to your
engagement JSON. The pipeline picks it up automatically on the next run.

Hybrid option (not yet implemented):
Use Tier 1 simple prompts for stage 1 (scoring) and Tier 2 rich prompts for
stage 2 (reasoning) only. This would preserve paper-validated PI scores while
still getting rich qualitative reasoning. To implement: in persona_generator.py,
set persona.system_prompt from the basic template and persona.reasoning_prompt
from the rich narrative. The pipeline already uses these two prompts separately.
