Our Method

Research-grade methodology,
radically faster execution

Our pipeline is built on published academic research in LLM-based consumer simulation. Real statistical rigor, real distributions, real insight.

01 — Brief

Share your concept

Send us your product concept — text, packaging, ad creative. 15-minute intake call to align on demographics and category.

02 — Simulate

Synthetic panel generation

We generate 100+ demographically-targeted synthetic consumers with realistic psychographic profiles — shopping habits, health orientation, brand adoption style.

03 — Elicit

Free-text response scoring

Each synthetic respondent reacts in their own words. Responses are scored into calibrated Likert distributions using semantic similarity.

04 — Deliver

Polished report

Presentation-ready deck with purchase intent distributions, demographic breakdowns, qualitative themes, and consumer objections.

Built on published research. Our scoring method (Semantic Similarity Rating) achieves 90% of the maximum correlation ceiling set by human test-retest reliability — validated with real consumer panel benchmarks.
